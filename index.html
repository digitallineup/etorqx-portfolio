Why this is the right portfolio for Etorqx

The Apex buyer and the Etorqx rider are the same household, doing the same maths.

  1. We have already sold energy maths on camera.

    Bijli bills, backup hours, fuel versus charge. The content that moved batteries is the content that moves e-bikes: real people, real numbers, real streets.

  2. We can run creators city by city.

    Sourcing, conflict checks, scripting, whitelisting for ads, and on-ground production. Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Multan already on the map; new dealer cities become launch moments, not address cards.

  3. One visual system across a product range.

    Apex's 1300 to 16K family is designed to be read at a glance. Zippy to Explorer needs exactly that: a model finder people understand in the feed.

  4. Design and production under one roof.

    Identity, posters, reels, films, websites and paid media come from the same team in Multan. No hand-offs between agencies, no drift in the look.
